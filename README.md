## 🚀 Featured Projects

Three production-level apps built during my time at Elevate Tech, spanning AI integration, e-commerce, and real-time logistics.

### 🏋️‍♂️ [Super Fitness & AI Coach App](https://github.com/Flutter-Elevate-Team2/Fitness)
> A wellness application powered by a smart AI personal trainer, with dynamic workout tracking and offline nutrition logs.
- **Tech Stack:** `Flutter` `Dart` `Gemini AI API` `BLoC` `Hive` `Firebase Auth` `Clean Architecture`
- **Key Features:** Real-time AI response streaming, personalized coaching plans, local data persistence with Hive, and solid Unit & Widget Testing (`bloc_test`, `mocktail`).

### 🌸 [Flowery (E-Commerce Platform)](https://github.com/Flutter-Elevate-Team2/Flowers-App)
> A premium flower delivery e-commerce app with a dynamic catalog, reactive cart, and secure checkout.
- **Tech Stack:** `Flutter` `Dart` `BLoC` `Google Maps SDK` `Mapbox` `Stripe` `Firestore`
- **Key Features:** Seamless Stripe checkout, live delivery tracking with automatic Google Maps → Mapbox fallback on non-GMS devices, and stable 60 FPS scrolling/pagination.

### 🛵 [Flowery Rider (Driver Tracking App)](https://github.com/Flutter-Elevate-Team2/Tracking-App)
> A high-performance companion app for delivery drivers, syncing live location to the backend in real time.
- **Tech Stack:** `Flutter` `Dart` `BLoC` `Firebase Firestore` `GoRouter` `Dio`
- **Key Features:** Persistent foreground service for uninterrupted GPS syncing, custom navigation lifecycle guards with GoRouter, and centralized network interceptors for retries & offline resilience.
